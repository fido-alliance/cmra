# Contributing Feedback to FIDO Alliance

Thank you for your interest in improving FIDO Alliance's Credential Manager Reference Architecture. This repository collects public feedback as well as hosts the working versions of the architecture. This document explains how to submit feedback and what happens after you do.

By participating in this project, you agree to follow our [Code of Conduct](COC.md).

## How Public Feedback Works

Public feedback is accepted **through GitHub issues**. 

This architecture is developed by FIDO Alliance working groups. Working group members will review public feedback and decide whether and how to address it in future versions of the architecture.

If you are a FIDO Alliance member, you can take part directly in the architecture development through your working group. 

If you are not a member and would like to take part more directly, please goto our website [FIDO Alliance](https://fidoalliance.org/alliance-membership/) to find out more about FIDO Alliance membership and our mission.

## Before You Submit a New Issue

- **Search existing issues** to see if your feedback has already been raised. If it has, add a comment or a 👍 reaction instead of opening a duplicate.
- **Check that you are looking at the latest version** of the architecture.
- **Do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues.** See [Reporting Security Issues](#reporting-security-issues) below.
- **Do not include confidential or proprietary information** in your feedback. Everything posted in this repository is public.

## Types of Feedback We Welcome

- **Technical issues** — errors, ambiguities, or inconsistencies in the architecture that could impact implementations or interoperability
- **Clarification requests** — sections that are unclear or open to more than one interpretation or do not provide enough context 
- **Editorial issues** — typos, broken links, formatting problems, or unclear wording
- **Suggestions** — ideas for improvements or new capabilities in future versions, including implementation experience that could inform the architecture or instances where the architecture maybe in conflict with regulatory guidelines

Please open one issue per topic. Separate issues are easier to discuss, track, and resolve.

## What to Include in an Issue

Include as much of the following as you can:

- **Document name and version** e.g. "[DOCUMENT NAME] v[X.Y], [publication date]"
- **Section number and title**, with a link to the relevant section (if applicable)
- **Description of the issue** — what the problem is and why it matters
- **Proposed resolution**, if you have one, such as suggested wording or an alternative approach
- **Context** — for example, whether you found the issue while building an implementation, and whether it affects interoperability

Please use the issue templates provided.

## What Happens Next

1. **Triage** — Working group participants review new issues.
2. **Discussion** — Reviewers may ask follow-up questions in the issue. Please check back and respond when you can.
3. **Working group review** — The relevant working group considers the issue. Discussion within the working group is confidential to FIDO Alliance members, so not all deliberations will appear in the issue.
4. **Resolution** — Where possible, the issue will be updated with the outcome and closed. Outcomes may include a change in a future version of the architecture, no change (with an explanation where possible), or a referral to a different forum.

Please note:

- Response times vary with working group schedules and document release cycles. 
- We may not be able to respond to every issue individually.
- Issues that are duplicates, off-topic, inactive, or outside the scope of this repository may be closed.
- The working group makes the final decision on all changes to the architecture.
